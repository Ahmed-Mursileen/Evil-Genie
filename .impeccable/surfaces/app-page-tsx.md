# Surface: home (wish → twist)

Scope: `/` (wish form, twist reveal, history) and `/t` (shared twist). Mode: Experience. The visitor is inside a toy: they make a wish, read the doom, and share it.

Audience and job: Pakistani youth on a phone at night (often during load shedding) type a wish, get a twist, and send it to the group chat. Constraints: cheap phones and slow connections, reduced motion respected, Roman Urdu mixed into UI copy, eldritch-being voice.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The genie advertises itself the way every Pakistani street does: an amil-baba wall chalking promising "har murad poori" on a dark cracked wall at night. The twist is the fine print painted underneath. It refuses the category default of a glowing lamp floating on a purple gradient card.

OWN-WORLD: Night plaster wall in violet-black (#15101d) with blotches, cracks and spray overspray. Paint colours are marigold gold (#f2b632) and chalking violet (#a472ff), with bone whitewash (#efe6d8) for the fine print. Lettering is sprayed/brushed (Rubik Spray Paint display, Barlow Condensed for the painted body) with an Urdu Nastaliq "ہر مراد پوری". Controls are painted blocks and chalk underlines, never rounded SaaS pills. The King in Yellow mood repaints the wall black with sick yellow paint (#e3c62b).

STORY: Visitors understand at once that this wall grants any wish. They write a wish in the painted box, pick a mizaj (mood), and press "Murad maango". The lamp rattles and the wall "wakes"; the twist sprays onto the wall line by line. They copy or share it and try again.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At mobile 390px, top to bottom: the Urdu line in gold spray, a huge "EVIL GENIE" chalked in violet with a gold drop-paint, the tagline "Har murad poori. Qeemat baad mein.", the wish box as a painted panel filling the width, a row of mood chips as chalk labels, and a full-width gold painted "Murad maango" block within the first screen. The lamp is a small painted chirag beside the title.

FORM: Amil-baba wall chalking (grounded list position 3 of 7, chosen by the user). Seed key 1e1b2373. Roll degraded (no challengers).

Signature interaction: the spray reveal. The twist appears via a masked left-to-right spray sweep with overspray settling, while the lamp shakes and smoke rises from it during loading.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
